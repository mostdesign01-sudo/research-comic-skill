# Research layouts

The dates and examples below are research leads, not a verified claim ledger. Check relevant primary sources for the current task and record evidence before including them in final output.

## Timeline
Use 4–6 selected milestones; dates above, illustration in the middle, one causal or capability change below. Date ranges are not exact invention dates. HTML: 1989 proposal / 1991 public description; 1993 graphical browser; 1990s standardization; 2014 HTML5; living standard; AI-assisted authoring is a workflow change, not a new HTML version.

## Solar system
Eight planets in order: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune. Pluto is a dwarf planet. Display diagram is not to scale. Prefer a factual DOM list beside an evocative engraving; inspect generated planet count carefully.

## Human evolution
Represent branching and coexistence, not a straight ape-to-human procession. Homo sapiens originated in Africa about 300,000 years ago. Distinguish fossil evidence from artist reconstruction. Do not assign direct parent-child ancestry where uncertain.

## AI history
Select field formation (1956), chess milestone (1997), deep-learning image recognition (2012), Transformer (2017), conversational public adoption (2022). This is selective history, not all AI research. Newest model superiority needs dated benchmarks and task context.

## Country comparison
Separate lanes, independent dates, explicit selection criterion. Examples: Qin unification 221 BCE; Edo government 1603; Magna Carta 1215. Do not imply chronological events in different lanes represent equivalent social systems or a progress ranking.
